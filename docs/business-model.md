# Business Model: Independent Farm & Industrial Machinery Repair Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7233`
- ISCO-08: `7233`
- Occupation: Agricultural and Industrial Machinery Mechanics and Repairers
- Social impact: local-jobs, worker-safety, rural-livelihoods

## Customer

- farmers
- small manufacturers
- equipment rental operators

## Offer

- diagnostic scanning
- repair quoting
- part replacement
- post-repair testing
- invoicing

## Revenue

- diagnostic call-out fee
- per-repair fee
- monthly practice platform fee

## Trust Controls

- no lift operation or pressurized-hydraulic work without governor gate
- test records are auditable, not editable
- quotes and invoices require sign-off
